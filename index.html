The Problem

Large retailers invest millions in attracting customers and selling home products, but the customer experience doesn't end with the sale. When installation or other field services are required, retailers rely on fragmented provider networks, leading to limited visibility, poor traceability, SLA breaches, and inconsistent customer experiences.

The LiLi Solution

LiLi enables retailers to orchestrate and scale their field services through a technology platform connected to a qualified network of professionals. We manage the entire service journey from assignment and scheduling to execution, evidence and real-time traceability helping retailers deliver consistent customer experiences at scale. Our B2B2C model combines enterprise platform fees with a commission on executed services.

Investment Thesis

The infrastructure layer enabling retailers and insurers to scale field services across Latin America.

What started with Liliana’s leaking roof became a mission to make every service at home more trusted, transparent, and effortless.

1. A structural problem in a proven market

Global players such as IKEA and Taskrabbit have validated the opportunity to orchestrate independent professionals at scale. Latin America adds a larger opportunity due to its highly fragmented and informal field service market.

2. Formalization as a competitive advantage

LiLi gives professionals free digital tools, faster payments, insurance, and administrative support, creating a more reliable and traceable professional network. More services → more data → better operations.

3. B2B2C led regional expansion

nstead of spending heavily on B2C acquisition, LiLi enters new markets through enterprise customers that already generate service demand. Enterprise demand → LiLi infrastructure → local professional network.

4. Aligned incentives, scalable economics

  • LiLi Pros: get more work and tools.
  • Enterprises: get coverage, control, and traceability.
  • Customers: get greater certainty and trust.

LiLi captures value through service volume and recurring enterprise platform revenue.

Revenue Model

B2B2C Enterprise Channel Primary Growth Engine

Retailers, insurers and assistance companies channel the service demand they already generate installations, maintenance, home services — through LiLi's managed professional network. LiLi executes the service and earns a share of it.

  • Revenue model: 25–28% + VAT commission on every service completed. No subscriptions or licensing fees (for now): this is currently the only source of revenue.
  • Average Ticket: ~$47,78 USD per service.
  • LiLi Pro Network: 580+ verified professionals connected nationwide.
  • Value Proposition to Partners: Hire nobody. No payroll. LiLi absorbs the operational execution.
  • Enterprise Integrations: API, webhooks, ERP and CRM integrations (SAP, Oracle, Salesforce roadmap).

On-Demand Service Layer

Customers can also request services through LiLi’s app, web or WhatsApp AI. This on-demand channel keeps the professional network active between enterprise jobs and provides additional demand, while LiLi’s primary growth strategy remains enterprise partnerships.

iOS / Android App WhatsApp AI liliapp.cl

Why Channel > Direct

  • Enterprise partners already own the customer relationship and the demand — LiLi doesn't have to buy it.
  • One signed retailer unlocks hundreds of jobs/month, vs. acquiring consumers one at a time.
  • Same 25–28% take rate on every job regardless of channel — volume through partners is what moves CAC down and margin up.

The Scalability Math (Per Enterprise Partner)

A single Tier-1 retailer with ~700 services/month, a ~US$48 average ticket, and a 25–28% take rate can generate approximately US$403K in annual GMV and US$101K–113K in annual transaction revenue for LiLi, excluding platform fees and value-added services.

The real technical magic is convergence. Regardless of the entry channel, everything funnels into a single platform (LiLi Pro):

  • AI WhatsApp Channel: customers request services 100% through natural-language conversation with our AI.

2. The Math Engine (B2B2C "Uber")

We don't rely on black-box AI to assign work — we run a robust predictive model:

  • Priority Algorithm: tiers based on NPS, experience and verification risk.
  • Game Theory & Rejections: repeatedly declining jobs instantly lowers a pro's priority.
  • Risk-Free Testing: 14-day payment escrow lets retailers trial technicians with zero financial risk.
